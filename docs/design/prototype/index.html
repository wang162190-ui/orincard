What the numbers mean

Projects, titles, dates Illustrative demo data from the snapshot’s projects.html. Not real business records.
Prices, quotas, commission Invented. spec §11 leaves all of them undecided; each figure on the billing screen carries an ILLUSTRATIVE marker.
Everything that “runs” A static script moving a progress bar. No generation, save, charge or download is real, and none of it is evidence the product does these things.
Download retention 30 days everywhere, derived from the snapshot’s own export record — exported Aug 24, expires Sep 23.
