## Review status

This draft describes intended product rules for human review. It is not a legally reviewed agreement and must not be used as production terms.

## Accounts and acceptable use

Users are responsible for account access and for content they submit. The service must not be used to violate law, impersonate others, bypass access controls, distribute malware, or submit material without the rights needed for the requested processing.

## User content and generated results

Users retain the rights they hold in submitted content. They grant only the permissions needed to operate requested features. Generated drafts may contain mistakes and require user review. Orincard does not claim that a generated post was published and does not guarantee that generated material is accurate, unique, or suitable for a particular purpose.

## Third-party assets

Stock media, fonts, music, AI images, and external services may carry separate terms. Users must review displayed rights information and accept generated candidates before applying or exporting them where the product requires confirmation.

## Plans and billing

Paid checkout and billing management use a hosted provider. The production terms must state approved prices, taxes, renewal timing, failed-payment treatment, plan limits, and the exact effective date. A cancellation scheduled at period end does not delete projects or cancel a separate account deletion request.

## Refund draft

No refund promise is approved in this draft. The final refund rule must state eligibility, request timing, mandatory consumer rights, partial-period treatment, and how a refund affects granted or consumed service entitlements.

## Availability and termination

Features may fail or change, and provider outages can block generation or export. The final terms must define notice, suspension, termination, warranty disclaimers, liability limits, dispute terms, and governing law appropriate to the operating entity and user region.

## Review questions

- Confirm the contracting entity and eligibility requirements.
- Approve renewal, cancellation, downgrade, and refund language.
- Approve warranty, liability, indemnity, dispute, and governing-law clauses.
- Confirm notice and policy-change procedures.
