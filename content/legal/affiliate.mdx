## What this policy covers

This policy governs the Orincard affiliate programme. It forms part of the Terms of Service. The version identifier at the top of this page governs each attributed conversion; a conversion is always settled under the policy version in force on the day it was attributed, not the version in force when it is paid.

## Joining

Anyone with an Orincard account can apply. Applications are reviewed manually and are usually answered within 10 business days. We can approve, reject, suspend, or close an affiliate account.

You must give accurate information about the channels you will promote on. You may not refer yourself, refer accounts you control, make claims about the product we have not published, impersonate Orincard, buy ads on Orincard trademarks or close misspellings of them, post your referral link on coupon or cashback sites without written permission, or use unsolicited email or messaging.

## Attribution

Attribution needs all of the following: the visitor accepted attribution tracking, the referral code is valid and active, the visit is within the attribution window, and your affiliate account is in good standing at the moment of conversion.

The attribution window is 30 days from the tracked visit. If a user is referred by more than one affiliate within that window, the most recent qualifying referral is credited.

You never receive a referred user's email address, project content, payment details, or private billing information. You see counts, conversion events, and the commission they generate.

## Commission

- Rate: 20 percent of the net amount Orincard actually receives for a referred subscription, after taxes, discounts, and payment processing fees.
- Duration: recurring for 12 months from the first paid invoice of that subscription. Payments after month 12 do not generate commission.
- Currency: United States dollars.
- Payout threshold: USD 50. Balances below the threshold roll over to the next cycle.
- Schedule: a commission becomes approved 45 days after the invoice it relates to is paid, which covers the refund and chargeback window. Approved balances above the threshold are paid on the 15th of the following month.

Commission states are pending, approved, paid, reversed, rejected, and suspended. Each state change is recorded as a new event against the original commission, so history is never rewritten in place.

## Reversals

A pending or approved commission is reversed when the underlying payment is refunded, disputed, charged back, or found to be fraudulent, when the attribution turns out to be a self-referral or a duplicate, or when the referred account is closed for breaking the Terms of Service. If the commission was already paid, the amount is deducted from your next payout.

## Disclosures and brand use

You must disclose that your link is a paid affiliate link, clearly and close to the link, in the way the advertising law where you publish requires. You may use only the brand assets we publish, without altering them. You may not state prices, discounts, or product outcomes we have not published, and you may not present yourself as an employee, agent, or representative of Orincard.

## Payouts and tax

Payouts are made through Stripe to the account you connect. You must complete the identity and tax information Stripe asks for before the first payout; we cannot pay out without it. You are responsible for your own taxes on what you earn, and for telling us if your tax residence changes.

## Ending participation

You can leave the programme at any time. Approved balances above the threshold are paid in the next cycle; pending balances are cancelled. We can end your participation for breaking this policy, and in that case unpaid balances connected to the breach are cancelled. We give the reason in writing.

## Changes

We may change this policy, including the rate, duration, window, threshold, and schedule. Changes take effect 30 days after we publish them and apply only to conversions attributed on or after the effective date.

## Contact

Questions about the programme go through the Support page in the product.
