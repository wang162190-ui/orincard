## Review status

This is an operational draft for human and legal review. It is not an approved affiliate offer, commission promise, or production policy.

## Participation draft

Applications may be approved, rejected, suspended, or closed under an approved policy. Applicants must provide accurate channel information and may not create misleading claims, impersonate Orincard, use prohibited advertising methods, or refer themselves.

## Attribution draft

Attribution should require user consent, an approved referral code, a defined attribution window, and a valid affiliate account at the time of attribution. Affiliates should not receive a referred user's email, project content, payment credentials, or private billing details.

## Commission draft

No commission rate, currency, threshold, schedule, or payment promise is approved here. The final policy must identify the policy version that governs each attributed conversion and explain pending, approved, paid, reversed, rejected, and suspended states.

## Refunds and reversals

Refunds, disputes, chargebacks, fraud, duplicate attribution, and ineligible self-referrals may reverse a pending or previously recognized commission according to the approved policy. Reversals must reference the original commission event rather than silently changing historical records.

## Disclosures and brand use

Affiliates must make legally required advertising disclosures and use only approved brand material. They may not promise product outcomes, publish unapproved prices or discounts, bid on restricted marks, or imply employment or agency authority.

## Review questions

- Approve eligibility, attribution window, commission calculation, and currency.
- Approve refund window, reversal, dispute, and fraud procedures.
- Approve payout provider, tax verification, minimum threshold, and schedule.
- Confirm required disclosures, regional restrictions, and termination rights.
