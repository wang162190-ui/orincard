## Draft status

This directory is a review workspace. Its contents are not legal advice, have not been approved by counsel or the product owner, and must not be presented as production policy.

## Publication gate

- Add separate Privacy, Terms, and Affiliate policy source files.
- Review data rights, retention, refunds, subscription cancellation, and commission language.
- Record explicit approval evidence for the exact committed text.
- Remove draft and no-index treatment only after approval.

## Product behavior

Until the publication gate passes, Orincard must label every legal route as a draft and prevent it from being indexed as an approved policy.
