## Review status

This is a product policy draft for human review. It has not been approved as legal advice or as Orincard production policy.

## Information covered by this draft

Orincard may process account identifiers, profile preferences, project documents, uploaded source files, generated assets, export records, usage records, support messages, and limited billing status. Payment card details remain with the hosted payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Orincard.

## How product data is used

Account and project data is used to authenticate the user, provide requested generation and editing features, save versions, create exports, restore user-confirmed packages, enforce quotas, prevent abuse, and answer support requests. A support request includes diagnostic IDs only when the user explicitly selects them.

## Providers and access

Selected content may be sent to configured infrastructure, AI, media, email, storage, and payment providers when required for a feature the user requests. The production policy must identify approved providers, processing locations, and applicable contractual safeguards before publication.

## Retention and deletion

Source files, generated candidates, and downloadable exports may use product-specific expiry periods. Saved projects and accepted assets remain until the user deletes them or the account deletion process reaches the relevant cleanup stage. Backups, billing audit records, fraud controls, and legal obligations may require separate limited retention. Exact periods require approval before publication.

## User controls

Users can review project versions, export an account data package, delete projects, and request staged account deletion. Restoration creates new private identifiers and does not revive another account's object keys. Requests about access, correction, deletion, or portability can be sent through Support.

## Review questions

- Confirm every retention period and lawful basis for each operating region.
- Confirm international transfer and subprocessors language.
- Confirm contact identity, controller details, age limits, and complaint rights.
- Confirm which deletion exceptions are legally required.
