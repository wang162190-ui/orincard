---
title: Turn source text into a clear carousel
description: Shape one useful argument into an editable sequence without losing its meaning.
---

## Find the promise

Read the source once and write the single change a reader should understand. Use that promise for the cover instead of copying the first sentence.

## Build the sequence

1. Open with the outcome or tension.
2. Give each middle page one job.
3. Keep evidence beside the claim it supports.
4. End with one practical next step.

## Edit the generated draft

Treat generation as a starting point. Remove repeated ideas, check every quotation, and shorten pages that need smaller type to fit. Apply images and Brand Kit choices only after the words are clear.

## Export for the destination

Choose the LinkedIn, Instagram, or TikTok preset before the final review. Run preflight, inspect the page order, and keep an editable or recovery-enabled copy when the work needs to be reused.
