---
title: Billing, waitlist, and cancellation
description: Understand the current waitlist state and how future paid-plan changes affect saved work.
---

## Paid access is not open

Orincard currently shows a waitlist entry while the signup platform, plan prices, allowances, and launch regions are being finalized. The unconfigured form does not submit or store an email.

## Existing billing records

Accounts used during sandbox testing may show a plan state, billing period, and usage balance. Test billing does not represent a live charge or a public price commitment.

## Cancellation keeps projects

Scheduling cancellation changes access at the end of the current paid period. It does not delete projects, exports, Brand Kits, or account data. Account deletion is a separate confirmed action.

## Payment details stay with the provider

When paid access opens, checkout and payment-method changes use the hosted payment provider. Orincard does not store card numbers or payment credentials.
